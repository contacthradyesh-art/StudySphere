STUDYSPHERE — ETHICS LAB (NEW FEATURE, GS4)
==============================================

Ethics Lab didn't exist before — it was just a "Coming soon" disabled card
on the Mission IAS hub. This builds a full v1:

1. CASE STUDY PRACTICE
   - "Generate Case Study" -> AI writes a fresh, realistic UPSC GS4
     Section-B style ethical dilemma (120-180 words) + the standard 3
     sub-questions (identify issues, list options, choose & justify).
   - You write your answer, submit, get AI feedback: score /100, ethical
     issues identified, options evaluated, strengths, improvements, and a
     concise model-approach to compare against.
   - Every attempt is saved (Firestore) and awards +15 XP into your
     existing Total XP.
   - Recent attempts history shown at the bottom.

2. CONCEPTS (searchable glossary)
   - 16 core GS4 terms: Ethics, Integrity, Probity, Aptitude vs Attitude,
     Emotional Intelligence, Foundational Values, Accountability,
     Transparency, Objectivity, Impartiality vs Non-partisanship,
     Corruption, Whistleblowing, Code of Ethics vs Conduct, Conscience,
     EQ vs IQ — each with a definition + why/how it shows up in the exam.

3. THINKERS & QUOTES
   - 10 thinkers relevant to GS4: Kant, Aristotle, Bentham/Mill, Gandhi,
     Kautilya, Confucius, Rawls, Socrates, Vivekananda, Amartya Sen — each
     with their core theory, a short quotable line, and exam relevance.
   - "More facts for GS4" button routes to your AI Assistant for deeper
     material on that thinker.
